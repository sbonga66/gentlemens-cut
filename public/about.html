Our story

Craft over noise.

A small Durban studio with a simple idea: great barbering should feel personal, precise and consistent.

The beginning

Built for the chair.

The Gentlemen's Cut opened in 2018 with two chairs and a focus on doing fewer things exceptionally well. Today the studio remains intentionally personal: skilled barbers, considered service and no unnecessary fuss.

Our approach combines classic barber traditions with contemporary cuts. Whether you know exactly what you want or need a recommendation, the consultation comes first.
